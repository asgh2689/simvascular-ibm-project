# Project Plan

## Phase 1: Literature Review

### Goals

* Understand the mathematical formulation of immersed boundary methods (IBM)
* Review applications of IBM in cardiovascular biomechanics
* Study existing FSI methodologies used in cardiovascular biomechanics
* Identify advantages and limitations of IBM compared to other mesh-based approaches

---

## Phase 2: Numerical Investigation

### Goals

* Implement simple IBM benchmark problems
* Explore Eulerian-Lagrangian coupling strategies
* Investigate force spreading and velocity interpolation methods
* Evaluate numerical stability considerations

---

## Phase 3: SimVascular Integration Study

### Goals

* Review SimVascular simulation architecture
* Identify potential integration points for IBM
* Investigate compatibility with patient-specific vascular models

---

## Long-Term Goals

* Prototype a basic IBM framework
* Explore moving-boundary cardiovascular simulations
* Evaluate feasibility for future SimVascular development

---

## Current Status

Active literature review and project planning.
